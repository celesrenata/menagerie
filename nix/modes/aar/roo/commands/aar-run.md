Start the AA&R lead-collection pilot. The time box is the hours given in the arguments (default 24); the lanes are those given (default: all lanes in aar-source-playbooks). Load the skills aar-mandate, aar-evidence-schema, aar-source-playbooks and omniroute-routing, then follow your workflow from Setup. Discovery only: no buying, bidding, outreach, accounts or logins.
