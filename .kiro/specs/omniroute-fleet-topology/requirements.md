# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature defines a ground-up routing structure for the OmniRoute inference fleet. The goal is to make bringing a new GPU online, retiring a model, or retuning a card a declarative, low-touch operation. The operator describes devices, serving endpoints, models, and lanes once, in one inventory. Routes, concurrency limits, client-facing route metadata, drift checks, and per-card observability are all derived from that inventory.

Current state, measured over 2026-10-07T04:24Z → 2026-10-14T04:24Z. Method, caveats, and full tables are in `.agents/tasks/omniroute-fleet-topology/findings.md`.

- **Fleet.**
  - The RTX 5090 (vLLM) carries 66% of 11,899 chat attempts at 97.2% success.
  - The RTX 4070 Ti Super (Ollama in k8s, one resident model) carries 21% at 85.3%.
  - The M5 Max (GLM-5.3, Uzu, and MLX behind one proxy) carries 7% at 62.0%.
  - Four Intel Arc iGPUs serve OVMS embeddings (77.8% success) and rerank (100%).
  - Cloud tiers absorb the rest.
- **Routing is owned in pieces.**
  - The cluster has 94 combos, but only 11 were used as entrypoints.
  - 56 combos fall into 14 byte-identical groups.
  - Dead targets remain wired in and caused 96 failed attempts per week.
  - A hand-coded generator regenerates only a subset of combos and copies the rest from live.
  - 63 of 90 shared combos drifted from the 2026-10-04 snapshot, and 206 routing backup and state artifacts have accumulated.
- **Capacity and context are declared in many places.** Capacity is declared in at least seven places and served context windows in at least ten. Menagerie's hard-coded route context table disagrees with live on 14 of 17 entries, and its static capacity map disagrees on 3 of 5.
- **Two OmniRoute instances overlap.** A second, Mac-local instance publishes the same route names with different targets and enforces its own caps against the same cards.
- **Runtime faults surface only through forensics.** At capture time the 4070 Ti Super had been skipped as "slot busy" while idle for about six hours. Its overflow went to the slowest local backend.
- **Onboarding is expensive.** Adding or retuning a card touches about nine systems and fifteen or more files, followed by hand-written verification scripts.

The design phase will propose multiple GPU arrangements. This document defines the structure that makes any arrangement declarative (Requirements 1–18). It also defines the evaluation method, preservation floors, and improvement objectives every arrangement is measured against, using the baselines above (Requirements 19–21). Requirement 22 governs change safety and secret handling across all of them.

This spec interacts with sibling specs without redefining them:

- **capability-lanes-routing** defines Capability_Lane (`reader.fast`, `reader.deep`, `coder.primary`, `reasoning.escalation`), Served_Context_Window, and Model_Swap_Load_Latency. It also sets the non-goal that Menagerie must not hard-code GPU, VRAM, or model identity. This spec supplies the OmniRoute-side data that those definitions assign to OmniRoute.
- **elastic-parallel-execution** defines RouteCapability, RouteCapacity, Inference_Lease, and the BoundedElasticScheduler. This spec publishes capacity in the RouteCapacity shape and does not change scheduler mechanics.
- **resilient-context-recovery** consumes Served_Context_Window.
- **`docs/architecture/omniroute-integration-design.md`** makes OmniRoute the single owner of routing, tiering, and placement, with Menagerie as a thin client. That ownership is unchanged here.

### Non-Goals

- Choosing the GPU arrangement. That belongs to the design phase.
- Generating host serving configuration (vLLM flags, Ollama values, the M5 model proxy, OVMS). The Fleet_Inventory declares the expected values and the Reconciler verifies them, but host modules keep owning provisioning.
- Fixing the specific runtime defects in the findings: the 4070 Ti Super reservation leak, the indexer embedding failures, and the GLM-5.3 degradation. Detecting this class of defect is in scope (Requirement 18).
- Changing OmniRoute combo-scheduling semantics, or supporting more than one OmniRoute replica per instance. Target reservations are process-local today.
- Managing cloud provider accounts or billing.

## Glossary

- **Fleet_Topology_System**: The system specified here. It comprises the Fleet_Inventory, Inventory_Parser, Inventory_Printer, Inventory_Validator, Route_Compiler, Reconciler, Fleet_Observer, and Arrangement_Evaluator, plus the Menagerie_Client changes that consume Published_Route_Metadata.
- **Fleet_Inventory**: The single version-controlled declarative document describing every Device, Serving_Endpoint, Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, Lane_Definition, and OmniRoute_Instance that participates in routing.
- **Fleet_Inventory_Revision**: The identifier of the most recent version-control commit that changed the Fleet_Inventory document (Requirement 1 criterion 9). A document with uncommitted changes is marked uncommitted (Requirement 5 criterion 10).
- **Inventory_Parser**: The component that parses a Fleet_Inventory document into a Fleet_Inventory model.
- **Inventory_Printer**: The component that formats a Fleet_Inventory model back into a Fleet_Inventory document.
- **Inventory_Validator**: The component that checks a parsed Fleet_Inventory for referential, numeric, allocation, uniqueness, structural, route-name, role, and secret-handling errors.
- **Device**: A physical accelerator or unified-memory machine that performs inference, for example the RTX 5090, RTX 4070 Ti Super, M5 Max, or one Intel Arc iGPU.
- **Device_Slot_Ceiling**: The maximum number of concurrent generations a Device supports across all Model_Offerings on that Device.
- **Device_Allocation**: The number of a Device's Slots that the Fleet_Inventory allocates to one OmniRoute_Instance (Requirement 1 criteria 4 and 11). It bounds that OmniRoute_Instance's concurrent generations on the Device.
- **Serving_Endpoint**: A network-reachable inference server bound to one or more Devices, for example a vLLM switcher, an Ollama service, the M5 model proxy, or an OVMS server.
- **Model_Offering**: One model id served by one Serving_Endpoint on one Device, with a declared Served_Context_Window, slot count, Residency_Group, capability tags, and Performance_Rank per Slot. A Serving_Endpoint bound to several Devices, such as a proxy in front of several OVMS hosts, declares one Model_Offering per Device.
- **Slot**: One unit of concurrent generation capacity of a Model_Offering.
- **Residency_Group**: A set of Model_Offerings on one Device that cannot all be resident at once (for example, Ollama with one loaded model), so switching between members costs Model_Swap_Load_Latency.
- **Performance_Rank**: An integer from 1 to 1,000 declared for each Slot in the Fleet_Inventory, where 1 is the best rank and two Slots may share a value. It orders Slots by expected like-sized end-to-end latency. The Fleet_Observer checks it against measurements by input-size bucket (Requirement 18 criterion 4).
- **Cloud_Target**: A hosted provider model reachable through an OmniRoute connection, with a declared Spend_Class, Served_Context_Window, and capability tags.
- **Spend_Class**: The cost classification of a Cloud_Target, ordered `free` < `low-budget` < `paid`.
- **Lane_Definition**: A declarative description of one client-facing lane. It specifies required capability tags, minimum Served_Context_Window, target ordering policy, maximum Spend_Class, Cloud_Spill_Tiers, an optional maximum queue wait, and whether the lane fails closed when every eligible target is saturated.
- **Cloud_Spill_Tier**: One step of a Lane_Definition's cloud fallback, declared as one Spend_Class. It contains the eligible Cloud_Targets of exactly that Spend_Class. A tier with Spend_Class `free` is a free-only tier.
- **Public_Route**: A stable OmniRoute route name that clients select as a model id (for example `hybrid/code`), compiled from exactly one Lane_Definition.
- **Route_Alias**: An additional Public_Route name that resolves to the same Lane_Definition as another Public_Route.
- **Internal_Pool**: A generated nested combo used only as a building block of Public_Routes, not selectable by clients.
- **Diagnostic_Route**: An operator-declared route pinned to one Device for troubleshooting, published outside the client-selectable catalog.
- **Target_Identifier**: The stable identifier of a compiled target. It is derived only from the identity of the Slot or Cloud_Target the target represents (Requirement 5 criterion 3).
- **Expanded_Target_Sequence**: The ordered list of Target_Identifiers of a combo name, with each reference to another combo replaced, recursively, by that combo's Expanded_Target_Sequence (Requirement 10 criterion 3).
- **Route_Compiler**: The component that generates the Desired_Route_State for each Managed_Instance from the Fleet_Inventory.
- **Desired_Route_State**: The complete set of combos and Routing_Settings, including Published_Route_Metadata, that the Route_Compiler produces for one OmniRoute_Instance.
- **Live_Route_State**: The combos and Routing_Settings, including synced model lists, per-model context overrides, and Published_Route_Metadata, present in one OmniRoute_Instance at a point in time.
- **Published_Route_Metadata**: The per-name route kind (Requirement 4 criterion 11), route context window (Requirement 13), RouteCapacity record, Capability_Lane mapping, and Model_Swap_Load_Latency data (Requirement 14) that an OmniRoute_Instance publishes in its route catalog.
- **Routing_Setting**: An OmniRoute connection or instance setting that changes dispatch: connection active flag, base URL, default model, max concurrency, rate-limit and wait overrides, synced model list, per-model context overrides, Published_Route_Metadata, hard-concurrency providers, strict-system-message providers, chat admission limits, and request and stream timeouts.
- **Reconciler**: The component that detects drift, produces Change_Plans, and applies or rolls back changes to Managed_Instances.
- **Drift**: Any difference between the Desired_Route_State and the Live_Route_State, or between declared and advertised model ids or context windows.
- **Change_Plan**: The ordered list of combo and Routing_Setting creates, updates, and deletes, with before and after values, that would make a Live_Route_State equal its Desired_Route_State.
- **Route_Backup**: A checksummed snapshot of every combo and Routing_Setting of one Managed_Instance, captured before an apply.
- **Preflight_Check**: A read-only check of a Serving_Endpoint: reachability, authentication through a secret reference, advertised model ids, and served context where exposed.
- **OmniRoute_Instance**: One running OmniRoute deployment, for example the cluster instance or the Mac-local instance.
- **Managed_Instance**: An OmniRoute_Instance whose role in the Fleet_Inventory is `primary` or `secondary`.
- **Fleet_Observer**: The component that computes per-device and per-route metrics and raises runtime anomaly alerts from OmniRoute request metadata and logs.
- **Alert_Destination**: The address declared in the Fleet_Inventory to which the Reconciler, Fleet_Observer, and Arrangement_Evaluator write alerts and recommendations (Requirement 1 criterion 13).
- **Health_Probe**: A request that an OmniRoute_Instance sends to a Serving_Endpoint to check a connection, rather than on behalf of a client.
- **Admission_Wait**: The time from an OmniRoute_Instance beginning an attempt until it dispatches the attempt to the Serving_Endpoint or Cloud_Target (Requirement 17 criterion 3).
- **Upstream_Duration**: The time from the dispatch of an attempt until the attempt's completion. Admission_Wait plus Upstream_Duration equals the attempt's end-to-end latency.
- **Capacity_Class_Rejection**: An attempt that ended because a concurrency limit was reached, whether the limit was enforced by the Serving_Endpoint or by the OmniRoute_Instance: HTTP 409 busy, HTTP 503 at capacity, or HTTP 429 semaphore timeout or queue full.
- **Dead_Target**: A compiled target whose attempts all failed across at least 10 consecutive attempts, or across 24 hours with at least one attempt, ignoring attempts that ended in a Capacity_Class_Rejection or in a client disconnect (HTTP 499).
- **Capacity_Leak**: A condition in which an OmniRoute_Instance treats a target slot as busy while no attempt on that target is in flight.
- **Host_Preemption**: A workload outside OmniRoute taking a Device, for example ComfyUI preempting the RTX 5090 through the vLLM switcher.
- **Menagerie_Client**: Menagerie's OmniRoute integration: `src/api/providers/omniroute.ts`, the route-aware context window resolution in `src/api/providers/openai.ts`, the parallel-worker route resolution in `src/core/task/parallelWorkerRouting.ts`, the route capacity provider in `src/core/task/routeCapacityMap.ts`, and the OmniRoute settings section.
- **Arrangement**: A candidate assignment of Model_Offerings to Devices, with Performance_Ranks and Lane_Definitions, expressed as a Fleet_Inventory_Revision.
- **Arrangement_Evaluator**: The component that measures an Arrangement against the Baseline_Metrics and the Preservation_Floors.
- **Evaluation_Window**: The period over which an Arrangement is measured, as Requirement 19 criterion 1 defines it. It starts once every Managed_Instance has applied the Arrangement, plus the longest request timeout. It ends at the earliest whole-hour offset from 7 days to 28 days at which each qualifying Public_Route has enough requests, or at 28 days.
- **Gated_Route**: A Public_Route of a Managed_Instance with at least 100 requests in the measured window. Preservation_Floors apply only to Gated_Routes.
- **Baseline_Metrics**: The metrics measured over 2026-10-07T04:24Z → 2026-10-14T04:24Z and recorded in findings.md section 9. They form the initial baseline.
- **Preservation_Floor**: A limit derived from a Baseline_Metric, either a minimum or a maximum, that a candidate Arrangement must satisfy to be accepted (Requirement 20).
- **Local_Demand_Distribution**: The fraction of local request starts that find 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 or more local attempts already in flight.
- **Capability_Lane**, **Served_Context_Window**, **Model_Swap_Load_Latency**: As defined in the capability-lanes-routing specification.
- **RouteCapability**, **RouteCapacity**, **BoundedElasticScheduler**: As defined in the elastic-parallel-execution specification.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Fleet inventory as the single source of truth

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want one declarative inventory of every device, serving endpoint, model, lane, and OmniRoute instance, so that a fleet change is made in one place instead of seven.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL declare each Device, Serving_Endpoint, Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, Lane_Definition, and OmniRoute_Instance that participates in routing as one entry with an identifier that is unique among entries of the same kind, where every OmniRoute_Instance holding at least one active connection to a Serving_Endpoint bound to a declared Device participates in routing, and a Device, Serving_Endpoint, Model_Offering, or Cloud_Target without an entry does not.
2. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Model_Offering: the Serving_Endpoint; the serving Device, which is one of the Devices the Serving_Endpoint is bound to; the model id, character for character as the Serving_Endpoint advertises it; the Served_Context_Window in tokens; the slot count; exactly one Residency_Group, whose members are all on the serving Device; the set of capability tags; and one Performance_Rank for each Slot, so that the number of Performance_Ranks equals the slot count, where each Performance_Rank is an integer from 1 to 1,000, 1 is the best rank, a larger value is a worse rank, and two Slots may share a value.
3. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Device, the Device_Slot_Ceiling as a positive integer and, for each Residency_Group on the Device that contains two or more Model_Offerings, the declared Model_Swap_Load_Latency as an integer number of milliseconds from 0 to 600,000.
4. WHERE more than one Managed_Instance connects to Serving_Endpoints bound to the same Device, THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record the number of that Device's Slots allocated to each of those Managed_Instances as an integer from 0 to the Device's Device_Slot_Ceiling, with each Managed_Instance omitted from the recorded allocation holding 0 Slots on that Device.
5. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Cloud_Target, the OmniRoute provider through which the Cloud_Target is reached, the model id character for character as that provider names it, the Spend_Class as exactly one of `free`, `low-budget`, or `paid`, the Served_Context_Window in tokens, and the set of capability tags.
6. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Device, an explicit `true` or `false` value stating whether the Device is subject to Host_Preemption.
7. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Managed_Instance, every Routing_Setting value either as a directly declared value or through the other Fleet_Inventory entries from which the Route_Compiler derives that value, with no Routing_Setting value recorded both ways.
8. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL reference each credential, including each API key, bearer token, password, and token-bearing URL path segment, by secret name or secret file path only, so that the Fleet_Inventory document contains no credential value.
9. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL be stored as one version-controlled text document at one path in one repository, with each Fleet_Inventory_Revision identified by the identifier of the most recent version-control commit that changed the document.
10. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Serving_Endpoint, the one or more Devices to which the Serving_Endpoint is bound, the OmniRoute provider used to connect to the Serving_Endpoint, the network address at which each Managed_Instance that connects to the Serving_Endpoint reaches it, and either the secret reference of the credential that authenticates to the Serving_Endpoint or an explicit statement that the Serving_Endpoint requires no credential.
11. THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL treat a Device that has no recorded Slot allocation as allocating a number of Slots equal to the Device's Device_Slot_Ceiling to the `primary` OmniRoute_Instance and 0 Slots to every other OmniRoute_Instance.
12. IF a Fleet_Inventory entry omits a field that this Requirement requires for that entry, or records a value outside the range or set of values that this Requirement states for that field, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the entry identifier and the field path.
13. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL declare exactly one Alert_Destination, as an address with any credential that it needs referenced by secret name or secret file path only, to which the Reconciler, the Fleet_Observer, and the Arrangement_Evaluator write the alerts and recommendations that Requirements 8, 18, and 21 specify.
14. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each OmniRoute_Instance, the role, the network address at which the Reconciler reaches the OmniRoute_Instance, and the credential reference or no-credential statement that Requirement 10 criterion 1 requires.
15. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL record, for each Managed_Instance, the Route_Backup retention count that Requirement 9 criterion 13 uses, as an integer from 1 to 1,000.
16. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL declare the order of the improvement objectives that Requirement 20 criterion 7 uses to rank Arrangements, listing each objective identifier of Requirement 20 criterion 6 exactly once.

### Requirement 2: Inventory parsing, printing, and validation

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want the inventory parsed and validated before any component uses it, so that a mistake is caught before it reaches live routing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a syntactically valid Fleet_Inventory document is provided to the Inventory_Parser, THE Inventory_Parser SHALL produce a Fleet_Inventory model that contains every declared Device, Serving_Endpoint, Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, Lane_Definition, and OmniRoute_Instance and every declared field name and value, including field names and values that the Inventory_Validator rejects.
2. IF a Fleet_Inventory document is syntactically invalid, THEN THE Inventory_Parser SHALL return, instead of a Fleet_Inventory model, an error that states the 1-based line and column of the first position at which the document cannot be parsed and names the kind of syntax element that is unexpected or missing, without including any field value from the document.
3. WHEN a Fleet_Inventory model that the Inventory_Validator accepts is provided to the Inventory_Printer, THE Inventory_Printer SHALL produce a Fleet_Inventory document that the Inventory_Parser parses without error, and SHALL produce byte-identical documents from equal Fleet_Inventory models.
4. FOR ALL Fleet_Inventory models that the Inventory_Validator accepts, printing with the Inventory_Printer and then parsing with the Inventory_Parser SHALL produce a Fleet_Inventory model equal to the original in every entity and field value, including the element order of every ordered field (round-trip property).
5. IF a Fleet_Inventory field references a Device, Serving_Endpoint, Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, Lane_Definition, or OmniRoute_Instance identifier that the Fleet_Inventory does not declare, or a Model_Offering names a Device to which the Model_Offering's Serving_Endpoint is not bound, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the referenced identifier and the field path of the reference (the sequence of field names and entry identifiers or list indices from the document root to the field).
6. IF a slot count or Device_Slot_Ceiling is not an integer from 1 through 64, a Slot allocation to an OmniRoute_Instance is not an integer from 0 through 64, or a Served_Context_Window or a Lane_Definition's minimum Served_Context_Window is not an integer from 1 through 16,777,216, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the field path and the permitted range.
7. IF the sum of the Slots allocated to all OmniRoute_Instances for one Device is greater than that Device's Device_Slot_Ceiling, one Model_Offering's slot count is greater than the Device_Slot_Ceiling of the Model_Offering's Device, or, for one Model_Offering, the sum over all OmniRoute_Instances of the lesser of the Model_Offering's slot count and that OmniRoute_Instance's Device_Allocation (Requirement 7 criterion 3) is greater than the Model_Offering's slot count, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the Device, the Device_Slot_Ceiling, and either the allocation sum, the Model_Offering and its slot count, or the Model_Offering and the sum of the lesser values.
8. IF two or more Model_Offerings on one Serving_Endpoint and one Device declare the same model id, two or more Devices, Serving_Endpoints, Cloud_Targets, Lane_Definitions, or OmniRoute_Instances declare the same identifier, or one name is declared as a Public_Route or Route_Alias name by two or more Lane_Definitions, comparing model ids, identifiers, and names as exact case-sensitive strings, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the field path of every entry that shares the duplicated value.
9. IF a field that the Fleet_Inventory format designates as a credential field (an API key, token, password, or authorization header value) holds a value other than a secret name or secret file path in the Fleet_Inventory format's secret-reference syntax, or a URL in any Fleet_Inventory field contains a password in its user-information part or a tokenized path segment of the kind that Requirement 22 redacts, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the field path without including the field value, or a masked or truncated form of it, in any output of the validation run.
10. WHEN the Inventory_Validator runs on a Fleet_Inventory model, THE Inventory_Validator SHALL return from that single run either an acceptance with zero violations or a rejection that lists every violation of every rule that this specification assigns to the Inventory_Validator, stating the field path and the kind of violation for each.
11. IF a Fleet_Inventory omits a value that this specification requires the Fleet_Inventory to record or declare, declares for a Model_Offering a number of Performance_Ranks different from the Model_Offering's slot count, or contains a field name that the Fleet_Inventory format does not define, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the field path of each missing value, mismatched Performance_Rank list, and undefined field name.
12. IF the Inventory_Parser returns an error for a Fleet_Inventory document or the Inventory_Validator rejects a Fleet_Inventory, THEN THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL withhold that Fleet_Inventory from the Route_Compiler, Reconciler, Fleet_Observer, and Arrangement_Evaluator, return the parse error or validation report as the result of the operation that loaded the Fleet_Inventory, and perform no write to any OmniRoute_Instance in that operation.

### Requirement 3: Capability-based lane definitions

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want lanes defined by required capabilities and policies instead of device names, so that a new device joins every lane it qualifies for without lane edits.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL define each Lane_Definition with: 1 to 32 required capability tags, each a non-empty string of at most 64 characters; a minimum Served_Context_Window that is a positive integer number of tokens no greater than the largest Served_Context_Window declared for any Model_Offering or Cloud_Target in the Fleet_Inventory; one target ordering policy from the set of ordering policies the Route_Compiler supports, which includes fill-first; one maximum Spend_Class of `free`, `low-budget`, or `paid`; a list of 0 to 8 Cloud_Spill_Tiers, each declared as one Spend_Class that is no higher than the Lane_Definition's maximum Spend_Class and that selects the Cloud_Targets of exactly that Spend_Class, where an empty list means the lane uses no Cloud_Target; a fail-closed declaration of yes or no; and, for a lane not declared fail-closed, an optional maximum queue wait of 0 to 120 seconds, which is 30 seconds when omitted.
2. IF a required capability tag of a Lane_Definition equals, ignoring letter case, a Device identifier, a Serving_Endpoint identifier, or a model id of a Model_Offering or Cloud_Target declared in the same Fleet_Inventory, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the Lane_Definition, the field, and the matched identifier.
3. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL declare each of the eight Public_Route names preserved by Requirement 4 (`hybrid/code`, `hybrid/research`, `hybrid/fast`, `hybrid/planner`, `hybrid/reader`, `hybrid/reviewer`, `hybrid/frontier`, `hybrid/tester`) in exactly one Lane_Definition, as that Lane_Definition's Public_Route name or as one of its Route_Alias names.
4. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL map each of the four Capability_Lanes (`reader.fast`, `reader.deep`, `coder.primary`, `reasoning.escalation`) to exactly one Lane_Definition, whether or not another Capability_Lane maps to the same Lane_Definition.
5. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL contain at least one Lane_Definition that requires a capability tag carried only by Model_Offerings that serve embedding requests, and at least one Lane_Definition that requires a capability tag carried only by Model_Offerings that serve rerank requests.
6. WHERE a Lane_Definition is declared fail-closed, IF, for a request on the lane's Public_Route, every target compiled into that Public_Route and permitted by the request's per-request tier ceiling, if any, is saturated, THEN THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL, within 1 second after the later of the request's arrival and the request's last Capacity_Class_Rejection, if any, either dispatch the request to one of those targets that has stopped being saturated or return an error response indicating that every target of the lane is saturated, counting a target as saturated for a request while a compiled concurrency limit that applies to the target on that OmniRoute_Instance is reached or once the target has answered that request with a Capacity_Class_Rejection.
7. IF a Lane_Definition omits a field that criterion 1 requires or declares a value that violates a constraint in criterion 1, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the Lane_Definition, each offending field, and the constraint violated.
8. IF one of the eight names in criterion 3 is declared in zero Lane_Definitions or in more than one, a Capability_Lane is mapped to zero Lane_Definitions or to more than one, or the Fleet_Inventory maps a name that is not one of the four Capability_Lanes in criterion 4 to a Lane_Definition, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report each missing, duplicated, or unknown name with every Lane_Definition that declares or maps it.

### Requirement 4: Stable public route namespace

**User Story:** As a Menagerie user, I want the route names my modes and workers send to stay stable while the hardware behind them changes, so that a fleet change never requires editing my profiles.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Route_Compiler runs against a valid Fleet_Inventory, THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish, in the Desired_Route_State of each Managed_Instance, exactly one Public_Route for each Lane_Definition, named with that Lane_Definition's first declared name.
2. WHEN the Route_Compiler runs against a valid Fleet_Inventory, THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish each of the following names, with identical spelling and letter case, as a Public_Route in the Desired_Route_State of each Managed_Instance: `hybrid/code`, `hybrid/research`, `hybrid/fast`, `hybrid/planner`, `hybrid/reader`, `hybrid/reviewer`, `hybrid/frontier`, and `hybrid/tester`, which are the Public_Routes used during the 2026-10-07T04:24Z → 2026-10-14T04:24Z baseline window.
3. WHERE a Lane_Definition declares more than one name, THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish each name after the first as a Route_Alias of that Lane_Definition's Public_Route in the Desired_Route_State of each Managed_Instance.
4. WHEN the Menagerie_Client loads an OmniRoute route catalog that carries Published_Route_Metadata, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL offer for selection exactly the Public_Routes and Route_Aliases that the Published_Route_Metadata lists, and no Internal_Pool, Diagnostic_Route, or other combo name.
5. WHERE the Fleet_Inventory declares a Diagnostic_Route, THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish the Diagnostic_Route outside the client-selectable catalog, with targets limited to Slots of Model_Offerings on the Device to which the Diagnostic_Route is pinned, in the Desired_Route_State of each Managed_Instance that serves that Device.
6. WHEN the Route_Compiler runs against a valid Fleet_Inventory that declares a retired name (a former Public_Route or Route_Alias name declared with a successor Public_Route), THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish the retired name as a Route_Alias of the successor Public_Route in the Desired_Route_State of each Managed_Instance.
7. THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report, over a selectable window of 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days, the count of requests whose requested model id is the name of an Internal_Pool or a retired Route_Alias, per requested name and per client API key name (never the key value), excluding Internal_Pool executions reached through a Public_Route.
8. IF a Fleet_Inventory contains any of the following route-name violations, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report each violation with the location of every declaration involved:
   - a Lane_Definition that declares no name;
   - a name declared more than once across Lane_Definition names, retired names, and Diagnostic_Route names;
   - a name listed in criterion 2 that is not the first declared name of a Lane_Definition;
   - a retired name whose successor is not the first declared name of a Lane_Definition in the same Fleet_Inventory;
   - a declared name that is not 1 to 64 characters long or that contains a character other than lowercase ASCII letters, digits, `/`, `-`, `_`, and `.`.
9. IF a Change_Plan would delete from a Managed_Instance a name that a previously applied Desired_Route_State published as a Public_Route or Route_Alias, and that name has not been published on that Managed_Instance as a retired Route_Alias continuously for at least 30 days (720 hours), THEN THE Reconciler SHALL abort any apply of that Change_Plan before the first write and report each such name with either the earliest time its deletion is allowed or the fact that it has not been retired.
10. IF an OmniRoute route catalog that the Menagerie_Client loads carries no Published_Route_Metadata, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL offer every entry of that catalog for selection.
11. THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish, in the Published_Route_Metadata of each Managed_Instance, the kind of every combo name in that Managed_Instance's Desired_Route_State as exactly one of `public`, `alias`, `internal`, or `diagnostic`, so that criterion 4 lists exactly the names of kind `public` and `alias` and the Menagerie_Client can classify any other name under Requirement 15 criterion 2.

### Requirement 5: Deterministic route compilation

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want every combo generated from the inventory, so that the live route set is reproducible and no combo is hand-maintained.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Route_Compiler compiles a valid Fleet_Inventory (one that the Inventory_Parser parses and the Inventory_Validator accepts with zero violations), THE Route_Compiler SHALL produce exactly one Desired_Route_State for each Managed_Instance and none for any OmniRoute_Instance whose role is `retired`, each containing every Public_Route, Route_Alias, and Diagnostic_Route that the Fleet_Inventory defines for that Managed_Instance, every Internal_Pool those routes reference directly or through other Internal_Pools, every Routing_Setting that the Fleet_Inventory declares or Requirement 7 derives for that Managed_Instance, the Published_Route_Metadata that Requirements 4, 13, and 14 define for that Managed_Instance, and no other combo.
2. FOR ALL valid Fleet_Inventory models, compiling the same model at the same Fleet_Inventory_Revision twice with the same Route_Compiler version SHALL produce byte-identical Desired_Route_State for each Managed_Instance, including when the two compilations run in separate processes, at different wall-clock times, under different time zone and locale settings, with different secret values behind the Fleet_Inventory's secret references, while OmniRoute_Instances hold different Live_Route_States, or with every OmniRoute_Instance and Serving_Endpoint unreachable (determinism property).
3. THE Route_Compiler SHALL derive each Target_Identifier only from the identity of the Slot the target represents (the Slot's Device, Serving_Endpoint, model id, and position from 1 to the Model_Offering's slot count) or of the Cloud_Target the target represents (the Cloud_Target's provider and model id), so that every target representing one Slot or Cloud_Target carries the same Target_Identifier in every compilation, distinct Slots and Cloud_Targets never share a Target_Identifier, and changing a Slot's Performance_Rank, Served_Context_Window, capability tags, lane membership, or route position leaves its Target_Identifier unchanged.
4. THE Route_Compiler SHALL emit no two Internal_Pools in one Desired_Route_State that have the same Target_Identifiers in the same order and the same value for every combo setting that changes dispatch, so that Public_Routes that use the same combination of targets and policy share one Internal_Pool.
5. THE Route_Compiler SHALL emit only Internal_Pools that contain at least one target and that at least one Public_Route in the same Desired_Route_State references, directly or through a chain of other Internal_Pools.
6. THE Route_Compiler SHALL record, in every combo of each Desired_Route_State, the version-control revision identifier of the compiled Fleet_Inventory document.
7. IF a Lane_Definition compiled for a Managed_Instance has no target eligible under Requirement 6 in any of its local or cloud tiers on that Managed_Instance, THEN THE Route_Compiler SHALL return a failure result that names every such pair of Lane_Definition and Managed_Instance found in that compilation run, produce no Desired_Route_State for any Managed_Instance, and leave every previously produced Desired_Route_State unchanged.
8. WHEN the Route_Compiler compiles a valid Fleet_Inventory containing at most 32 Devices, 128 Model_Offerings, 512 Slots, 64 Cloud_Targets, 32 Lane_Definitions, 64 Route_Aliases, 32 Diagnostic_Routes, and 4 Managed_Instances, THE Route_Compiler SHALL produce the Desired_Route_State of every Managed_Instance within 10 seconds of wall-clock time, measured from the start of the compilation run.
9. IF the Inventory_Parser or the Inventory_Validator rejects the Fleet_Inventory given to the Route_Compiler, THEN THE Route_Compiler SHALL return a failure result that lists every error the Inventory_Parser or Inventory_Validator reported, produce no Desired_Route_State for any Managed_Instance, and leave every previously produced Desired_Route_State unchanged.
10. IF the compiled Fleet_Inventory document has uncommitted changes or is not under version control, THEN THE Route_Compiler SHALL mark the Fleet_Inventory_Revision recorded in every combo of each Desired_Route_State as uncommitted.
11. THE Route_Compiler SHALL emit no combo that references itself, directly or through a chain of other combos.

### Requirement 6: Target eligibility, ordering, and spill

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want each lane's target order computed from capability, context, and measured speed, so that a faster new device is used first and a slower device serves only as overflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Route_Compiler SHALL exclude from a lane every Slot of each Model_Offering, and every Cloud_Target, that lacks one or more of the capability tags the Lane_Definition requires, matching tags as exact, case-sensitive strings.
2. THE Route_Compiler SHALL exclude from a lane every Slot of each Model_Offering, and every Cloud_Target, whose Served_Context_Window is less than the Lane_Definition's minimum Served_Context_Window.
3. WHILE a Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list, as returned to the Reconciler during the current run, omits the exact model id of a Model_Offering that the Fleet_Inventory declares for that Serving_Endpoint, THE Reconciler SHALL, unless the Model_Offering's Device is declared subject to Host_Preemption, include in every Change_Plan of that run the removal of every target of that Model_Offering from every compiled route on every Managed_Instance, and SHALL report the Serving_Endpoint, the omitted model id, and each affected compiled route; for a Device declared subject to Host_Preemption, whose advertised model list can omit a model id transiently, the Reconciler SHALL report the omission and each route that the removal would affect and plan no removal.
4. WHERE a Lane_Definition uses fill-first ordering, THE Route_Compiler SHALL order the lane's local Slots by ascending Performance_Rank value, with Performance_Rank 1 as the best rank, placing each additional Slot of one Model_Offering at that Slot's own Performance_Rank and ordering Slots of equal Performance_Rank by Target_Identifier in ascending lexicographic order.
5. THE Route_Compiler SHALL place every Cloud_Spill_Tier of a lane after every local Slot of that lane, in ascending order of the tier's Spend_Class, keeping the Lane_Definition's order among tiers of equal Spend_Class.
6. THE Route_Compiler SHALL include in each Cloud_Spill_Tier of a lane exactly the Cloud_Targets whose Spend_Class equals the tier's Spend_Class and that carry every capability tag and meet the minimum Served_Context_Window that the Lane_Definition requires, and SHALL report each Cloud_Target of that Spend_Class that it excluded for a missing tag or an insufficient Served_Context_Window, with the tier's Lane_Definition.
7. WHERE a Cloud_Spill_Tier has Spend_Class `free` (a free-only tier), IF every Cloud_Target in that tier has failed or been skipped for a request on a compiled route containing the tier, and no later tier of that route contains a target that criterion 8 permits for the request, THEN THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL end the request with an error indicating that the tier's free Cloud_Targets are exhausted or unavailable, within 1 second after the last of them fails or is skipped, without queuing the request or dispatching a further attempt.
8. THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL dispatch every attempt for a request on a compiled route only to a target that is compiled into that route and permitted by the request's `X-OmniRoute-Tier` value, where value 1 permits local Slots; value 2 permits local Slots and Cloud_Targets of Spend_Class `free`; value 3 permits local Slots and Cloud_Targets of Spend_Class `free` or `low-budget`; and value 4 or 5, an absent header, or any value other than an integer from 1 to 5 permits every target compiled into the route.
9. THE Route_Compiler SHALL include in a lane every Slot of each Model_Offering that carries every capability tag the Lane_Definition requires and whose Served_Context_Window is greater than or equal to the Lane_Definition's minimum Served_Context_Window.
10. WHERE a Lane_Definition uses fill-first ordering, WHILE at least one local Slot of the lane is neither reserved nor already failed for a request, WHEN that request arrives on a Public_Route or Route_Alias of the lane or an attempt for that request fails, THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL dispatch the request's next attempt to the first such local Slot in the order defined by criterion 4.
11. THE Route_Compiler SHALL exclude from every lane each Cloud_Target whose Spend_Class is higher than the Lane_Definition's maximum Spend_Class.

### Requirement 7: Capacity and queue policy derived from slot declarations

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want every concurrency limit derived from the inventory's slot declarations, so that a card's capacity is set once and enforced identically on every route.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Route_Compiler SHALL compute each concurrency limit of an OmniRoute connection to a local Serving_Endpoint, and each per-target combo concurrency value of a local target (a compiled target that represents a Slot of a Model_Offering), only from the Fleet_Inventory's slot counts, Device_Slot_Ceilings, per-OmniRoute_Instance Slot allocations, and the Device and Serving_Endpoint of each Model_Offering, so that changing only Lane_Definitions, capability tags, Performance_Ranks, Served_Context_Windows, Cloud_Targets, Diagnostic_Routes, or the Live_Route_State leaves each such value unchanged for every connection and local target present both before and after the change.
2. THE Route_Compiler SHALL compile each Managed_Instance's Desired_Route_State so that the OmniRoute_Instance never has more in-flight attempts on a Model_Offering than the lesser of the Model_Offering's slot count and that OmniRoute_Instance's Device_Allocation (criterion 3), counted across every Public_Route, Route_Alias, Internal_Pool, and Diagnostic_Route of that OmniRoute_Instance, where an attempt is in flight from its dispatch to the Serving_Endpoint until its response completes, fails, or is cancelled.
3. THE Route_Compiler SHALL compile each Managed_Instance's Desired_Route_State so that the OmniRoute_Instance never has more in-flight attempts on a Device than its Device_Allocation, counted across every Model_Offering and Serving_Endpoint on the Device and including requests that name a direct provider model id, where the Device_Allocation is the number of Slots that the Fleet_Inventory allocates to that OmniRoute_Instance on the Device under Requirement 1 criteria 4 and 11 (zero allowed).
4. WHEN the Route_Compiler compiles a Fleet_Inventory in which two or more Lane_Definitions are eligible for the same Model_Offering, THE Route_Compiler SHALL draw every such lane's targets on that Model_Offering from one shared Slot budget per Managed_Instance, so that a request on any of those lanes can reserve every Slot that no other attempt holds and a Slot held through one lane is unavailable to the other lanes.
5. WHEN a request on a Public_Route reaches a local target for which the bounds in criteria 2 and 3 leave no unreserved Slot on that OmniRoute_Instance, THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL advance the request to the next eligible target in the Public_Route's compiled order within 1 second after reaching the saturated target, without dispatching the request to the saturated target.
6. THE Route_Compiler SHALL list the provider of every local Serving_Endpoint in the Fleet_Inventory in the hard-concurrency providers Routing_Setting of every Managed_Instance's Desired_Route_State, so that each Managed_Instance skips a saturated local target instead of admitting an attempt beyond the concurrency limit of that target's connection.
7. IF drift detection finds, on an OmniRoute connection of a Managed_Instance, a concurrency limit, rate-limit override, or wait override whose live value differs from the Desired_Route_State or that the Desired_Route_State does not contain, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report the setting as Drift, naming the Managed_Instance, the connection, the setting, the live value, and the desired value or its absence.
8. IF every eligible target of a Public_Route whose Lane_Definition is not declared fail-closed is saturated, meaning no eligible local target has an unreserved Slot under criteria 2 and 3 and every eligible Cloud_Target's connection is at its concurrency limit, THEN THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL hold the request for up to the maximum queue wait that the Fleet_Inventory declares for that Lane_Definition (0 to 120 seconds, or 30 seconds when the Lane_Definition declares none), without dispatching the request to a saturated target and without returning a capacity error before that wait elapses.
9. IF the maximum queue wait of a request held under criterion 8 elapses while every eligible target of the request's Public_Route is still saturated, THEN THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL return an error indicating that the lane's capacity is exhausted within 1 second after the wait elapses, without dispatching the request to any target.

### Requirement 8: Drift detection

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want a report of every difference between the inventory and each live OmniRoute instance, so that hand edits and stale state are visible before they cause incidents.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN drift detection runs, THE Reconciler SHALL classify each difference between the Desired_Route_State and the Live_Route_State of each Managed_Instance, matching objects by name, as exactly one of:
   - missing: the object is in the Desired_Route_State and absent from the Live_Route_State;
   - extra: the object is in the Live_Route_State, records a producing Fleet_Inventory_Revision, and is absent from the Desired_Route_State;
   - changed: the object is in both, and at least one compared field differs in value or exists in only one of the two; a different order of entries in a combo list is a difference, and a different order of entries in a Routing_Setting list is not;
   - unmanaged: the object is in the Live_Route_State, records no producing Fleet_Inventory_Revision, and is absent from the Desired_Route_State.
2. THE Reconciler SHALL include in drift detection every combo, connection, Routing_Setting, synced model catalog, and Published_Route_Metadata entry of each Managed_Instance, comparing every combo field except fields that the OmniRoute_Instance sets itself to record creation time, modification time, usage counts, or health status, and except the record of the producing Fleet_Inventory_Revision, which criterion 1 uses only to tell extra from unmanaged.
3. WHILE drift detection runs, THE Reconciler SHALL send only read requests to OmniRoute_Instances and Serving_Endpoints, triggering no write, model catalog synchronization, or inference request, so that a drift detection run leaves every Live_Route_State and the set of models resident on each Serving_Endpoint unchanged.
4. WHEN the operator requests drift detection, THE Reconciler SHALL run drift detection against the latest committed Fleet_Inventory_Revision, covering every Managed_Instance and Serving_Endpoint that the revision declares.
5. IF, during drift detection, a Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list contains a model id that the Fleet_Inventory does not declare for that Serving_Endpoint, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report the model id as undeclared, matching model ids by exact, case-sensitive comparison.
6. IF, during drift detection, a Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list omits a model id that the Fleet_Inventory declares for that Serving_Endpoint, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report the model id as missing, matching model ids by exact, case-sensitive comparison.
7. IF, during drift detection, a Managed_Instance's synced model catalog for a connection to a Serving_Endpoint differs from that Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list under exact, case-sensitive comparison, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report each model id that is advertised but absent from the catalog as missing, and each model id that is in the catalog but not advertised as stale.
8. WHERE a Serving_Endpoint exposes the context window that it enforces for a model, IF that value differs from the declared Served_Context_Window of the corresponding Model_Offering, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report the Model_Offering as changed with the exposed and declared values in tokens.
9. WHEN a drift detection run ends, THE Reconciler SHALL produce a machine-readable report and a human-readable summary, where:
   - both state the run start and end times, the trigger (operator request or schedule), the Fleet_Inventory_Revision compared, the outcome, and the number of differences in each classification (missing, extra, changed, unmanaged, undeclared, stale) for each Managed_Instance and Serving_Endpoint;
   - the outcome is `failed` if the run ended because the Fleet_Inventory failed parsing, validation, or compilation, otherwise `incomplete` if at least one value is unverified, otherwise `complete`;
   - the machine-readable report lists each difference with its Managed_Instance or Serving_Endpoint, its object name or model id, its classification, and each differing field, if any, with its desired and live values, redacted as Requirement 22 requires;
   - the machine-readable report lists each unverified value with its failure reason, and each declared value that was not compared because the target does not expose it.
10. IF a read from a Managed_Instance or Serving_Endpoint returns no successful, parseable response within 10 seconds on each of 3 consecutive attempts, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report every value covered by that read as unverified, with the failure reason (timeout, connection failure, authentication rejection, error response, or unparseable response), instead of classifying any of those values as a difference, and continue with the remaining reads and targets.
11. THE Reconciler SHALL start scheduled drift detection runs against the latest committed Fleet_Inventory_Revision, with at most 24 hours between the start times of consecutive scheduled runs.
12. IF the Fleet_Inventory_Revision of a drift detection run fails parsing or validation, or the Route_Compiler stops compilation of that revision, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL end the run without classifying any difference and report the run as failed with every parse, validation, and compilation error found.
13. WHEN a scheduled drift detection run ends with an outcome other than `complete` or with at least one difference, THE Reconciler SHALL write the run's human-readable summary to the Alert_Destination declared in the Fleet_Inventory within 60 seconds of the run ending.

### Requirement 9: Dry run, backup, apply, and rollback

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want every route change previewed, backed up, verified, and reversible, so that applying the inventory is safe on the production gateway.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Reconciler is invoked without apply or rollback mode selected, or without the per-invocation operator confirmation required by Requirement 22, THE Reconciler SHALL run in dry-run mode, in which the Reconciler produces the Change_Plan for each Managed_Instance the invocation targets, or for a rollback request the list of objects the rollback would write with each object's current value and Route_Backup value, and performs no writes to any OmniRoute_Instance or Serving_Endpoint.
2. THE Reconciler SHALL list in each Change_Plan the target Managed_Instance, the Fleet_Inventory_Revision, the creation time in UTC, and, for every combo and Routing_Setting to be created, updated, or deleted, the object identifier, the operation, the complete before value (marked absent for a create), and the complete after value (marked absent for a delete), ordered so that each combo creation precedes every write in the Change_Plan that adds a reference to that combo and each combo deletion follows every write in the Change_Plan that removes a reference to that combo.
3. WHEN a confirmed apply of a non-empty Change_Plan is requested, THE Reconciler SHALL capture a Route_Backup of the Change_Plan's Managed_Instance and verify the stored Route_Backup against its checksum before the first write.
4. IF, at the start of an apply, any object in the Live_Route_State of the Change_Plan's Managed_Instance differs from its value when the Change_Plan was created, comparing as Requirement 8 criterion 2 compares, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL abort the apply before the first write and report each differing object's identifier, its value at Change_Plan creation, and its current value.
5. WHEN the Reconciler writes a combo or Routing_Setting during an apply, a restore after a failed apply, or a rollback, THE Reconciler SHALL read the object back before issuing the next write, compare the readback value with the intended value (the Change_Plan after value for an apply write, or the Route_Backup value for a restore or rollback write), and record each apply write's readback value with the apply's Route_Backup as that object's post-apply value.
6. IF, during an apply, a write returns an error, a write or its readback does not complete within 30 seconds, or a readback differs from the Change_Plan after value, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL issue no further apply writes, restore every object the apply attempted to write, including the failed object, to its Route_Backup value (deleting any object absent from the Route_Backup) in the reverse of the order attempted, and report the failed object, the failure cause, and the restore result of each object.
7. WHEN a confirmed rollback to a Route_Backup is requested, THE Reconciler SHALL, before the first rollback write, verify the Route_Backup against its checksum and confirm that each object whose write was attempted by the apply that captured the Route_Backup equals either its recorded post-apply value or its Route_Backup value, and then restore each such object that differs from its Route_Backup value (deleting any object absent from the Route_Backup) in the reverse of the order in which the apply wrote them.
8. IF, for a rollback request, the Route_Backup is missing or fails checksum verification, or any object whose write was attempted by the apply that captured the Route_Backup equals neither its recorded post-apply value nor its Route_Backup value, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL refuse the rollback before the first write and report the Route_Backup failure or each drifted object's identifier, current value, recorded post-apply value, and Route_Backup value.
9. FOR ALL successfully applied Change_Plans, re-running the Reconciler in dry-run mode for the same Managed_Instance and Fleet_Inventory_Revision, with no intervening change to the Live_Route_State, the model ids advertised by Serving_Endpoints, or Preflight_Check results, SHALL produce an empty Change_Plan (idempotence property).
10. FOR ALL successfully applied Change_Plans, rolling back to the Route_Backup captured by that apply, with no change to the Live_Route_State between the end of the apply and the rollback, SHALL make every combo and Routing_Setting of the Managed_Instance equal its Route_Backup value, with every object the apply created absent (round-trip property).
11. IF the Change_Plan of a confirmed apply contains at least one combo deletion and the operator has not confirmed deletions for that apply invocation, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL abort the apply before the first write and report each combo deletion awaiting confirmation.
12. WHEN an apply or rollback ends, whatever its outcome, THE Reconciler SHALL write an audit entry containing the identity of the confirming operator, the start and end times in UTC, the Fleet_Inventory_Revision of the Change_Plan applied or rolled back, the number of creates, updates, and deletes planned and the number completed with a matching readback, the outcome (succeeded, no changes, aborted or refused before the first write, failed and restored, or failed with objects left unrestored), and the location of each Route_Backup captured or restored.
13. WHEN an apply that issued at least one write ends, THE Reconciler SHALL keep the most recent Route_Backups of that Managed_Instance by capture time, up to the retention count declared in the Fleet_Inventory (an integer from 1 to 1,000), and delete every older Route_Backup of that Managed_Instance.
14. WHEN the Reconciler applies a Change_Plan, THE Reconciler SHALL issue apply writes only for the combos and Routing_Settings listed in the Change_Plan that the operator confirmed, each with its listed after value, in the listed order.
15. IF the Route_Backup for an apply cannot be captured and verified against its checksum within 60 seconds, or an apply or rollback is requested while another apply or rollback is in progress on the same Managed_Instance, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL abort the requested apply or refuse the requested rollback before the first write and report the cause.
16. IF, during a restore after a failed apply or during a rollback, a write returns an error, the write or its readback does not complete within 30 seconds, or the readback differs from the Route_Backup value, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL issue no further writes in that invocation and report each object not yet restored with its current value and its Route_Backup value.
17. IF the Fleet_Inventory_Revision of a Change_Plan is marked uncommitted under Requirement 5 criterion 10, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL still produce the Change_Plan in dry-run mode, and SHALL refuse a confirmed apply of it before the first write and report the uncommitted Fleet_Inventory document.

### Requirement 10: OmniRoute instance roles

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want each OmniRoute instance's role declared, so that two gateways never disagree about what a route name means or double-book the same card.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Fleet_Inventory SHALL declare, for each OmniRoute_Instance, exactly one role, written as one of the lowercase values `primary`, `secondary`, or `retired`, together with the network address at which the Reconciler reaches that OmniRoute_Instance and either the secret reference of the credential that authenticates the Reconciler to it or an explicit statement that it requires no credential.
2. IF a Fleet_Inventory contains any of the following role violations, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report each violation with the field path of every role declaration or Slot allocation involved:
   - a number of OmniRoute_Instances declared `primary` other than one, reported also with the number found, which may be 0;
   - an OmniRoute_Instance that declares no role, more than one role, or a role other than `primary`, `secondary`, or `retired`, compared as exact case-sensitive strings;
   - an allocation of one or more Slots of any Device to an OmniRoute_Instance whose role is `retired`.
3. WHEN the Route_Compiler produces Desired_Route_States from a valid Fleet_Inventory that declares one or more `secondary` OmniRoute_Instances, THE Route_Compiler SHALL produce, for each `secondary` OmniRoute_Instance, a Desired_Route_State in which:
   - the set of Public_Route and Route_Alias names equals the set in the `primary` OmniRoute_Instance's Desired_Route_State, compared as exact case-sensitive strings;
   - each of those names resolves to the same Lane_Definition as in the `primary` OmniRoute_Instance's Desired_Route_State (the Lane_Definition that declares the name or, for a retired name under Requirement 4, the Lane_Definition of its successor Public_Route) and carries the same published route context window;
   - for each of those names, every Target_Identifier that appears in the Expanded_Target_Sequences of both OmniRoute_Instances appears in the same relative order, by first occurrence, in both, where a name's Expanded_Target_Sequence is its ordered list of Target_Identifiers with each reference to another combo replaced, recursively, by that combo's Expanded_Target_Sequence.
4. WHEN drift detection runs against a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that the Inventory_Parser parses, the Inventory_Validator accepts, and the Route_Compiler compiles without a failure result, THE Reconciler SHALL read each OmniRoute_Instance whose role in that revision is `retired`, treat its Desired_Route_State as empty, and report as Drift of that OmniRoute_Instance, in the machine-readable report, human-readable summary, and per-classification counts of Requirement 8 criterion 9, exactly the following objects, each once, classified `extra` if the object records a producing Fleet_Inventory_Revision and `unmanaged` otherwise, and listed with the declared name it reuses, if any, and each Device bound to a Serving_Endpoint that it matches or reaches under condition (a) or (b), or with an unidentified Device where it matches or reaches a connection only under condition (c):
   - each active connection that meets at least one of these conditions: (a) its base URL has the host and port of a network address that the Fleet_Inventory records for a Serving_Endpoint; (b) its provider is the OmniRoute provider that the Fleet_Inventory records for a Serving_Endpoint, and its default model or synced model list contains, by exact case-sensitive comparison, the model id of a Model_Offering of that Serving_Endpoint; (c) its provider is an OmniRoute provider that the Fleet_Inventory records for at least one Serving_Endpoint and for no Cloud_Target;
   - each combo with at least one target that, directly or through a chain of other combos, reaches a connection, active or not, that meets condition (a), (b), or (c), by naming that connection or, when the target names no connection, through the provider default;
   - each combo whose name equals, by exact case-sensitive comparison, a Lane_Definition name, retired name (Requirement 4), or Diagnostic_Route name that the Fleet_Inventory declares.
5. IF a Change_Plan, apply, or rollback is requested for an OmniRoute_Instance that the latest committed Fleet_Inventory_Revision, or the Fleet_Inventory_Revision from which the request's Change_Plan is or was produced, declares as `retired` or does not declare, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL refuse the request before producing a Change_Plan or sending any write request, leave that OmniRoute_Instance's Live_Route_State unchanged, and report the OmniRoute_Instance with the role that each of those revisions declares for it or the fact that a revision does not declare it.
6. IF, during drift detection, a read from a `retired` OmniRoute_Instance returns no successful, parseable response within 10 seconds on each of 3 consecutive attempts, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL report every value covered by that read as unverified with the failure reason (timeout, connection failure, authentication rejection, error response, or unparseable response), report no object covered by that read as Drift, and continue with the remaining reads and targets.

### Requirement 11: Onboarding a device as an inventory-only change

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want to bring a new GPU online by adding it to the inventory and reconciling, so that I no longer hand-edit generator code, combos, connection settings, catalogs, and Menagerie constants.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Reconciler produces a Change_Plan for a Managed_Instance from a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that declares a Device, Serving_Endpoint, or Model_Offering (a new entry) that the revision last successfully applied to that Managed_Instance does not declare, THE Reconciler SHALL include in that Change_Plan, subject to criteria 3 and 4:
   - the placement of every Slot of each new Model_Offering in each lane for which Requirement 6 criteria 1, 2, and 9 make the Model_Offering eligible, at the Slot's declared Performance_Rank;
   - the creation of each OmniRoute connection to a new Serving_Endpoint, or the update of the existing connection to it, with the concurrency limit that Requirement 7 derives;
   - the Routing_Settings that Requirement 7 criterion 6 and Requirements 4, 13, and 14 derive for the new entries, including the Published_Route_Metadata of every Public_Route and Route_Alias whose eligible Slots change.
2. THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL support onboarding a Device, a Serving_Endpoint, and Model_Offerings with an edit to the Fleet_Inventory document and the creation of any secret that the new secret references name, and with no edit to Route_Compiler source, Menagerie_Client source, an individual combo, or an individual connection, so that a drift detection run after a successful apply of the onboarding Change_Plan reports no Drift for any new entry.
3. WHEN a Change_Plan would contain a new Serving_Endpoint, THE Reconciler SHALL, before including any Model_Offering or connection of that Serving_Endpoint in the Change_Plan, run that Serving_Endpoint's Preflight_Check using only read requests and the credential resolved from its secret reference, applying the retry rule of Requirement 8 criterion 10 to each read, and SHALL evaluate these checks:
   - reachability: the Serving_Endpoint answers a read request at the address that the Fleet_Inventory records for the Managed_Instance;
   - authentication: the Serving_Endpoint accepts the credential, or the Fleet_Inventory states that it needs none;
   - model ids: the Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list contains, by exact case-sensitive comparison, the model id of each Model_Offering that the Fleet_Inventory declares for the Serving_Endpoint;
   - context window: where the Serving_Endpoint exposes the context window that it enforces for a model, the exposed value equals the declared Served_Context_Window of the corresponding Model_Offering.
4. IF a check of criterion 3 fails for a Serving_Endpoint, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL exclude from the Change_Plan every Model_Offering and connection of that Serving_Endpoint for a reachability or authentication failure, or only the Model_Offerings named by a model-id or context-window failure, SHALL compile the Change_Plan as if the excluded Model_Offerings were absent from the Fleet_Inventory (so that Requirement 5 criterion 7 applies if that leaves a Lane_Definition without an eligible target on the Managed_Instance), and SHALL report each failed check with the Serving_Endpoint, the check, the excluded Model_Offerings, and the failure reason, without any credential value.
5. WHEN a confirmed apply creates an OmniRoute connection to a new Serving_Endpoint, THE Reconciler SHALL create the connection inactive, repeat that Serving_Endpoint's Preflight_Check immediately before activating the connection, activate the connection only if every check passes, and treat a repeat that does not pass as a write that returned an error under Requirement 9 criterion 6.
6. WHEN a confirmed apply creates an OmniRoute connection, or its Change_Plan adds a Model_Offering to the Serving_Endpoint of an existing connection, THE Reconciler SHALL synchronize that connection's model catalog as a step of the Change_Plan, before activating a newly created connection, and SHALL read the synchronized model list back and compare it, by exact case-sensitive comparison, with the Serving_Endpoint's advertised model list, treating a difference as a readback mismatch under Requirement 9 criterion 6.
7. THE Reconciler SHALL order each onboarding Change_Plan so that, for each new or changed connection, its creation or update, its model catalog synchronization, and its activation precede every combo creation or update that adds a target using the connection, and every write that publishes route metadata referring to the new Slots.

### Requirement 12: Decommissioning and model replacement

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want removing or replacing a model to remove every reference to that model, so that dead targets stop costing failed attempts.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Reconciler produces a Change_Plan for a Managed_Instance from a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that omits a Model_Offering or Cloud_Target declared in the revision last successfully applied to that Managed_Instance, THE Reconciler SHALL include in that Change_Plan the removal of every target that resolves to the omitted Model_Offering or Cloud_Target from every Public_Route, Route_Alias, Internal_Pool, and Diagnostic_Route of that Managed_Instance, where a target resolves to a Model_Offering when it names the Model_Offering's model id and reaches the Model_Offering's Serving_Endpoint through a named OmniRoute connection or, if it names no connection, through the provider default, and resolves to a Cloud_Target when it names the Cloud_Target's provider and model id.
2. WHEN a Fleet_Inventory_Revision omits a Model_Offering that the preceding revision declares and adds a Model_Offering that carries exactly the same set of capability tags, THE Route_Compiler SHALL place every Slot of the added Model_Offering, each at its own declared Performance_Rank, in each lane whose required capability tags the added Model_Offering carries and whose minimum Served_Context_Window is at most the added Model_Offering's Served_Context_Window, on every Managed_Instance that serves the added Model_Offering's Device.
3. WHEN the Reconciler produces a Change_Plan for a Managed_Instance from a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that omits a Device or Serving_Endpoint declared in the revision last successfully applied to that Managed_Instance, THE Reconciler SHALL include in that Change_Plan the deactivation, without deletion, of every OmniRoute connection on that Managed_Instance whose base URL points at an omitted Serving_Endpoint or at a Serving_Endpoint all of whose Devices the revision omits, ordered after every combo update in that Change_Plan that removes a target using that connection.
4. WHEN the Reconciler produces a Change_Plan for a Managed_Instance from a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that omits a Model_Offering declared in the revision last successfully applied to that Managed_Instance, THE Reconciler SHALL include in that Change_Plan the removal of each synced model catalog entry for the omitted model id on a connection to the omitted Model_Offering's Serving_Endpoint and of each per-model context override for the omitted model id (unless another Model_Offering in the revision declares that model id), and SHALL report as stale, with that Change_Plan and naming the combo and the model id, each target that resolves to the omitted Model_Offering in a combo that the Route_Compiler did not generate.
5. IF a Fleet_Inventory_Revision omits a Device that, in the revision last successfully applied to a Managed_Instance, shares a Serving_Endpoint with at least one Device that the new revision still declares, THEN THE Reconciler SHALL keep every OmniRoute connection that the new revision declares active for that Serving_Endpoint active in that Managed_Instance's Change_Plan after values.
6. WHEN the Reconciler produces a Change_Plan for a Managed_Instance from a Fleet_Inventory_Revision that omits a Model_Offering declared in the revision last successfully applied to that Managed_Instance, THE Reconciler SHALL report, with that Change_Plan, each Public_Route whose count of local Slots usable on that Managed_Instance decreases, giving the count in the Live_Route_State and in the Desired_Route_State.
7. IF a Diagnostic_Route is pinned to a Device that the Fleet_Inventory does not declare, THEN THE Inventory_Validator SHALL reject the Fleet_Inventory and report the Diagnostic_Route and the undeclared Device.

### Requirement 13: Published route context windows

**User Story:** As a Menagerie developer, I want each route's served context window published by OmniRoute, so that auto-condense uses the real window without a hand-maintained table.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish, in the route catalog of each Managed_Instance, one route context window for each Public_Route and each Route_Alias, expressed as a positive integer number of tokens equal to the minimum Served_Context_Window of the Lane_Definition that the Public_Route or Route_Alias resolves to.
2. WHEN a route catalog load from an OmniRoute profile's saved server URL completes successfully, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL use each published route context window that is an integer from 1 to 16,777,216 tokens as the Served_Context_Window, in auto-condense and in every other Menagerie_Client consumer of Served_Context_Window, for each task and parallel worker that runs on a profile with that saved server URL and whose model id matches the Public_Route or Route_Alias name exactly and case-sensitively, starting with the first Served_Context_Window resolution that begins after the load completes.
3. IF the most recent successful route catalog load from the saved server URL of a task's or parallel worker's profile publishes no route context window for the route, publishes a value that is not an integer from 1 to 16,777,216 tokens, or has not occurred in the current extension session, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL resolve that route's Served_Context_Window from the first available of the built-in per-route constant for that route, the profile's context-window value, and the default context window of 128,000 tokens.
4. WHEN a successful route catalog load from a profile's saved server URL publishes, for a route, an integer route context window from 1 to 16,777,216 tokens that differs from the Served_Context_Window the Menagerie_Client is using for that route, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL use the new value from the first Served_Context_Window resolution that begins after the load completes, with no extension restart and no profile save.
5. THE Menagerie_Client SHALL give precedence over the built-in per-route constant and over the profile's context-window value, whether either is larger or smaller than the published value, only to route context windows from catalogs loaded with the profile's saved server URL and API key, and not to a catalog loaded with unsaved values in the OmniRoute settings section.
6. IF any Model_Offering or Cloud_Target compiled into a Public_Route, directly or through an Internal_Pool, has a Served_Context_Window smaller than the minimum Served_Context_Window of that Public_Route's Lane_Definition, THEN THE Route_Compiler SHALL stop compilation and report the Public_Route, the Lane_Definition's minimum Served_Context_Window, and each such target with the target's Served_Context_Window.
7. WHILE no route catalog load from an OmniRoute profile's saved server URL has succeeded in the preceding 60 minutes, WHEN a task or parallel worker starts on that profile, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL start a route catalog load from that server URL, with at most one load in flight per server URL, without delaying the first request of the task or parallel worker.
8. IF a route catalog load fails or does not complete within 10 seconds, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL keep using the route context windows from the most recent successful load from the same server URL in the current extension session, leaving every Served_Context_Window in use unchanged.
9. THE Route_Compiler SHALL derive every per-model context override, every combo context length, and every weighted-target maximum input-token policy in a Desired_Route_State only from the Served_Context_Window of the Model_Offering or Cloud_Target concerned, or, for a combo context length, from that combo's published route context window, so that changing a Served_Context_Window in the Fleet_Inventory changes every such value for that Model_Offering or Cloud_Target at the next compilation, and no other input changes them.

### Requirement 14: Published capacity and lane routes for the Menagerie scheduler

**User Story:** As a Menagerie developer, I want lane routes and route capacity published by OmniRoute, so that the scheduler admits workers against real slots and no capacity map has to be kept in lockstep by hand.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish exactly one RouteCapacity record for each Public_Route, whose capacity equals the number of distinct local Slots compiled as targets of that Public_Route, directly or through Internal_Pools, in the primary OmniRoute_Instance's Desired_Route_State, with each Device contributing at most the Slots the Fleet_Inventory allocates to the primary OmniRoute_Instance on that Device (the Device's Device_Slot_Ceiling where no allocation is recorded).
2. THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish a mapping that assigns each Capability_Lane exactly one Public_Route, namely the Public_Route compiled from the Lane_Definition that the Fleet_Inventory maps to that Capability_Lane, identified by the Public_Route's own name rather than a Route_Alias.
3. THE Route_Compiler SHALL publish, for each Residency_Group that contains more than one Model_Offering, the Model_Swap_Load_Latency that the Fleet_Inventory declares for that Residency_Group, in whole milliseconds, together with each Public_Route whose compiled targets include a Slot of a Model_Offering in that Residency_Group.
4. WHEN the Menagerie_Client builds the per-RouteCapability capacity map for a parallel batch from Published_Route_Metadata, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL set each RouteCapability capacity to the number of distinct local Slots compiled, in the primary OmniRoute_Instance's Desired_Route_State, as targets of the Public_Routes that the published Capability_Lane mapping assigns to the Capability_Lanes that the capability-lanes-routing specification maps to that RouteCapability for at least one task type, counting a Slot shared by two Public_Routes once and with each Device contributing at most the Slots allocated to the primary OmniRoute_Instance on that Device (the Device_Slot_Ceiling where no allocation is recorded).
5. IF the Menagerie_Client cannot retrieve Published_Route_Metadata within 5 seconds after a parallel batch starts, or the retrieved metadata does not map every Capability_Lane to a Public_Route, or lacks a RouteCapacity record with a non-negative integer capacity for a mapped Public_Route, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL build that batch's capacity map from the existing static route capacity map, including any per-RouteCapability capacity overrides the operator has set, and log a warning that states which of these conditions occurred.
6. WHERE published lane routing is enabled in the OmniRoute settings section, which is disabled by default, WHEN the Menagerie_Client resolves the model id of a parallel worker that uses an OmniRoute profile, has no per-worker route in its parallel task specification, and whose mode has no saved profile with a model id different from the parent's model id, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL use the published Public_Route of the worker's Capability_Lane as the worker's model id.
7. IF a parallel batch's capacity map is built from Published_Route_Metadata and a RouteCapability's distinct local Slot count is 0, including a RouteCapability to which no Capability_Lane maps, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL set that RouteCapability capacity to its value in the existing static route capacity map, including any per-RouteCapability capacity override the operator has set for it.
8. WHERE the operator has set a per-RouteCapability capacity override in Menagerie's existing parallel capacity setting, IF that override is lower than the RouteCapability's distinct local Slot count computed from Published_Route_Metadata, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL use the override value as that RouteCapability's capacity.
9. WHERE published lane routing is enabled, IF the Published_Route_Metadata for a parallel worker's batch was not retrieved within 5 seconds after the batch started, maps no Public_Route to the worker's Capability_Lane, or maps that Capability_Lane to a name absent from the route catalog's Public_Routes, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL resolve that worker's model id as it does while published lane routing is disabled and log a warning that names the Capability_Lane.

### Requirement 15: Route reference validation in Menagerie

**User Story:** As a Menagerie user, I want saved profiles that point at missing, internal, or direct-model routes flagged, so that misrouted modes are caught before they spill to paid cloud or fail.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a refresh of the OmniRoute route catalog succeeds with at least one Public_Route, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL flag each non-empty route reference (model id, reader role route id, reasoner role route id, or custom route model id) of a saved OmniRoute profile that does not exactly match, including letter case, any Public_Route or Route_Alias name in the refreshed catalog, checking only profiles whose server URL has the same origin (scheme, host, and port) as the refreshed catalog's server URL.
2. WHEN the Menagerie_Client flags a route reference or the codebase-index embedder model id, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL give the flag exactly one reason, in this precedence: `internal` for an id that the Published_Route_Metadata of Requirement 4 criterion 11 lists with kind `internal` or `diagnostic` for the refreshed catalog's OmniRoute_Instance, `direct-model` for an id that one of that OmniRoute_Instance's connections serves as a provider model id instead of a Public_Route, and `missing` for every other id.
3. WHILE the codebase-index embedder's base URL has the same origin (scheme, host, and port) as an OmniRoute route catalog's server URL, WHEN a refresh of that catalog succeeds with at least one Public_Route and the non-empty codebase-index embedder model id does not exactly match, including letter case, any Public_Route or Route_Alias name in that catalog, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL flag the codebase-index embedder model id, whether or not codebase indexing is enabled.
4. WHILE the OmniRoute settings section is displayed, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL show each current flag as text in that section, stating the saved profile name or the codebase-index embedder setting, the setting that holds the flagged id, the flagged id, and the flag reason.
5. WHILE a route reference or the codebase-index embedder model id is flagged, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL send each request that uses the flagged id with the id exactly as saved, without substituting another id and without blocking the request.
6. IF a refresh of the OmniRoute route catalog fails or returns no Public_Route, THEN THE Menagerie_Client SHALL keep every existing flag unchanged and show in the OmniRoute settings section that route references for that server URL origin were not validated, until a later refresh for the same origin succeeds with at least one Public_Route.
7. WHEN a refresh of the OmniRoute route catalog succeeds with at least one Public_Route, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL, within 1 second after receiving the catalog, replace every flag raised by an earlier refresh for the same server URL origin with the flags raised by that refresh.
8. WHEN the Menagerie_Client raises or removes a flag, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL leave the saved value of every profile and setting unchanged.

### Requirement 16: Per-device and per-route metrics

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want standing per-card and per-route metrics, so that I can see how each card performs and where each route lands without writing ad hoc scripts.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, which is the trailing 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days ending at the request time, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report for each Device and each Model_Offering:
   - attempt count;
   - success rate, as the percentage of attempts that succeeded;
   - failed-attempt counts by HTTP status and, within each status, by the error type that the OmniRoute_Instance recorded;
   - end-to-end latency p50 and p95 of successful attempts;
   - end-to-end output tokens per second p50 of successful attempts;
   - input and output token totals over all attempts;
   - peak in-flight concurrency, as the largest number of attempts in flight at any instant of the window;
   - time-weighted in-flight concurrency, as the in-flight attempt count averaged over the whole window duration.
2. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report, for each Model_Offering and each input-size bucket of 10,000–49,999, 50,000–99,999, and 100,000 or more input tokens, the sample count, end-to-end latency p50 and p95, and end-to-end output tokens per second p50 of the successful attempts in the window whose recorded input token count falls in that bucket, placing attempts with fewer than 10,000 input tokens or no recorded input token count in no bucket.
3. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report, for each Public_Route of each OmniRoute_Instance, over the requests in the window that named the Public_Route or one of its Route_Aliases:
   - request count;
   - final success rate, as the percentage of requests whose final attempt succeeded;
   - end-to-end latency p50 and p95 of finally successful requests, each measured from the start of the request's first attempt to the completion of its final attempt;
   - multi-attempt rate, as the percentage of requests with more than one attempt;
   - failed-attempt time p50 and p95, where each sample is the summed end-to-end latency of the failed attempts of one finally successful request that has at least one failed attempt;
   - landing distribution, as the percentage of finally successful requests whose final attempt was served by each Device and each Cloud_Target, and separately by each Model_Offering and each Cloud_Target;
   - cloud share, as the percentage of finally successful requests whose final attempt was served by a Cloud_Target;
   - cloud input and output token totals over every attempt of those requests that a Cloud_Target served, whether or not the attempt succeeded.
4. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report for each Device:
   - busy fraction for each in-flight level N from 1 through the greater of the Device's Device_Slot_Ceiling and the Device's peak in-flight concurrency, as the percentage of the window during which at least N attempts were in flight on the Device;
   - Capacity_Class_Rejection counts, in total and by HTTP status;
   - for each Residency_Group on the Device that contains more than one Model_Offering, the model-switch count, where a model switch is an attempt whose immediately preceding attempt on the same Residency_Group, ordered by start time, targeted a different Model_Offering;
   - for each Model_Offering in such a Residency_Group, the time-to-first-token p50 of successful attempts with a recorded time-to-first-token that are model switches minus the time-to-first-token p50 of successful attempts with a recorded time-to-first-token that are not.
5. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report the Local_Demand_Distribution as five percentages, for 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4 or more, that sum to 100% before rounding, treating the start of each generation attempt on a Device in the window as one local request start and counting the other generation attempts in flight on any Device at that instant, where generation attempts exclude embeddings and rerank attempts.
6. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a selected window, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report, for each pair of OmniRoute_Instance and Serving_Endpoint, the Health_Probe count, the Health_Probe rate in probes per hour of the window, and the probe failure rate as the percentage of those probes that the OmniRoute_Instance recorded as failed, where a Health_Probe is a request the OmniRoute_Instance sends to the Serving_Endpoint to check the connection rather than on behalf of a client.
7. IF a Serving_Endpoint has at least one attempt in the selected window with a recorded time-to-first-token and more than 10,000 input tokens, and more than 10% of those attempts report a time-to-first-token below 50 ms, THEN THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report every time-to-first-token metric of that Serving_Endpoint's Model_Offerings for that window, including the post-switch time-to-first-token differences in criterion 4, with a mark indicating that the metric is unreliable.
8. WHEN the operator requests metrics for a 7-day selected window covering up to 100,000 call-log rows, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report every metric in criteria 1 through 7 within 60 seconds of the request.
9. THE Fleet_Observer SHALL compute and report every metric in criteria 1 through 7 under these conventions:
   - each attempt is attributed as specified in Requirement 17, and Health_Probes count only toward criterion 6;
   - Device, Model_Offering, and Local_Demand_Distribution metrics combine the attempts recorded by every OmniRoute_Instance declared in the Fleet_Inventory;
   - an attempt belongs to the selected window when its completion time is at or after the window start and before the window end, and a request belongs to the window when its final attempt does;
   - an attempt's end-to-end latency is the time from the OmniRoute_Instance beginning the attempt, including any capacity-admission wait, to the attempt's completion;
   - an attempt's end-to-end output tokens per second is its output token count divided by its end-to-end latency in seconds;
   - an attempt is in flight from its completion time minus its end-to-end latency (inclusive) to its completion time (exclusive), and concurrency and busy-fraction metrics count only the part of each in-flight interval inside the window;
   - an attempt succeeded when its recorded HTTP status is a 2xx success status, and failed otherwise;
   - a request is the set of attempts that share one correlation id, and its final attempt is the attempt with the latest completion time;
   - p50 and p95 use the nearest-rank method and are reported with their sample counts;
   - every metric appears in both a machine-readable report and a human-readable summary, each stating the selected window's start and end times.
10. IF the Fleet_Observer cannot read the attempt or Health_Probe records of an OmniRoute_Instance, or neither that OmniRoute_Instance's own records nor the Fleet_Observer's retained copy (criterion 12) cover the selected window, THEN THE Fleet_Observer SHALL compute the metrics from the remaining records and report each metric that depends on that OmniRoute_Instance as partial, naming the OmniRoute_Instance and the time span, if any, that its readable records cover.
11. IF a percentile or percentage metric has no qualifying samples in the selected window, or a time-to-first-token difference has no samples on either side, THEN THE Fleet_Observer SHALL report that metric as having no data, with its sample counts, instead of a numeric value.
12. THE Fleet_Observer SHALL, at least once every 24 hours, copy from the attempt and Health_Probe records of each OmniRoute_Instance into Fleet_Observer storage every field that criteria 1 through 7 and Requirement 17 use (completion time, end-to-end latency, admission wait, upstream duration, HTTP status, error type, input and output token counts, time to first token, correlation id, requested model id, OmniRoute connection, upstream model id, Target_Identifier, client API key name, and Capability_Lane name), copying no request or response body and no credential value, and SHALL retain the copied fields for at least 90 days, so that criteria 1 through 7 cover windows longer than an OmniRoute_Instance's own record retention.

### Requirement 17: Attribution identity and admission-wait recording

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want every attempt attributed to a stable device, model, route, and lane, with the attempt's admission wait recorded, so that metrics stay comparable across route regenerations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Fleet_Observer SHALL attribute each attempt that an OmniRoute_Instance records as follows:
   - active revision: the Fleet_Inventory_Revision live on that OmniRoute_Instance at the attempt's start time (its completion time minus its end-to-end latency), where an apply on that OmniRoute_Instance whose audit entry (Requirement 9) records the outcome succeeded or no changes makes its Fleet_Inventory_Revision live from the apply's end time, a rollback whose audit entry records succeeded or no changes makes the revision that was live when the rolled-back apply started live again from the rollback's end time, and no other outcome changes the live revision;
   - a Health_Probe, as Requirement 16 defines it, is attributed only to the Serving_Endpoint that the active revision declares for the probed OmniRoute connection, and the remaining items apply to every other attempt;
   - Device and Model_Offering, or Cloud_Target: the Device and Model_Offering of the Slot, or the Cloud_Target, that the attempt's recorded Target_Identifier represents in the Desired_Route_State compiled from the active revision for that OmniRoute_Instance or, for an attempt recorded without a Target_Identifier, the Model_Offering or Cloud_Target that the active revision declares for the attempt's recorded OmniRoute connection and upstream model id; an attempt attributed to a Cloud_Target has no Device or Model_Offering, and an attempt recorded as not dispatched with neither a Target_Identifier nor an OmniRoute connection has no Device, Model_Offering, or Cloud_Target; and an attempt recorded without a Target_Identifier whose OmniRoute connection and upstream model id map to Model_Offerings on more than one Device has the Serving_Endpoint but no Device;
   - Public_Route: the Public_Route that the attempt's requested model id names in the active revision, as the Public_Route's own name or as one of its Route_Aliases, matched exactly including letter case; any other requested model id, such as the name of an Internal_Pool, a Diagnostic_Route, or a direct provider model id, gives the attempt no Public_Route;
   - Lane_Definition: the Lane_Definition from which the active revision compiles the attributed Public_Route, whatever Capability_Lane name the attempt records, and none when the attempt has no Public_Route;
   - Capability_Lane: the Capability_Lane whose name equals the name recorded with the attempt under criterion 3, and none when the attempt records no name.
2. IF an attempt has no active revision, or its active revision does not map the attempt's recorded Target_Identifier (or, when none is recorded, its recorded OmniRoute connection and upstream model id) to a Slot, Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, or, for a Health_Probe, Serving_Endpoint, THEN THE Fleet_Observer SHALL attribute the attempt's Device and Model_Offering, Cloud_Target, or Serving_Endpoint from the Fleet_Inventory_Revision that maps it and whose apply on the same OmniRoute_Instance started most recently at or before the attempt's start time, leaving the attempt's Public_Route, Lane_Definition, and Capability_Lane as criterion 1 determines them.
3. THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL record with each attempt that it makes on behalf of a client:
   - the OmniRoute connection and upstream model id to which it dispatched the attempt, or for which the attempt was waiting when it ended, and, for an attempt made through a combo, the Target_Identifier of that combo target;
   - the admission wait, in whole milliseconds, from the OmniRoute_Instance beginning the attempt until it dispatches the attempt to the Serving_Endpoint or Cloud_Target, recorded as 0 rather than left empty when there is no wait, where a request's first attempt begins when the OmniRoute_Instance receives the request, each later attempt begins when the request's previous attempt ends, and the wait includes any hold for a Lane_Definition's maximum queue wait (Requirement 7) and any wait for a chat admission limit, an OmniRoute connection's concurrency limit, or a rate limit;
   - the upstream duration, in whole milliseconds, from that dispatch until the attempt's completion, so that the admission wait plus the upstream duration equals the attempt's end-to-end latency within 1 millisecond;
   - the Capability_Lane name carried in the request's metadata, unmodified when it is at most 64 characters long and truncated to its first 64 characters otherwise, or no Capability_Lane name when the request carries none.
4. WHEN the Menagerie_Client sends a request to an OmniRoute_Instance for a task or parallel worker that has a Capability_Lane assigned at send time, including each retry and each request whose model id was set explicitly rather than resolved through the published Capability_Lane mapping, THE Menagerie_Client SHALL attach that Capability_Lane's name as request metadata, spelled exactly as the capability-lanes-routing specification defines it (`reader.fast`, `reader.deep`, `coder.primary`, or `reasoning.escalation`), and attach no Capability_Lane name to any other request.
5. IF the Fleet_Observer cannot determine an attribute of a recorded attempt under criteria 1 and 2 because no Fleet_Inventory_Revision was live on the recording OmniRoute_Instance at the attempt's start time, a revision the attribution needs cannot be read from version control, no revision searched under criteria 1 and 2 maps the attempt's recorded Target_Identifier or OmniRoute connection and upstream model id, the connection and upstream model id map to Model_Offerings on more than one Device, or the attempt's recorded Capability_Lane name is not one of the four names listed in criterion 4, THEN THE Fleet_Observer SHALL:
   - mark each attribute left undetermined as unattributed and keep the attempt's other attributes;
   - count the attempt in every metric that does not depend on an unattributed attribute;
   - show unattributed attempts and requests as a separate entry in every distribution across Devices, Model_Offerings, or Cloud_Targets;
   - report, for the selected window, the number of affected attempts per OmniRoute_Instance and per reason, and, for unmapped values, per distinct recorded Target_Identifier, OmniRoute connection and upstream model id, or Capability_Lane name.
6. FOR ALL recorded attempts, once every apply and rollback that started on the recording OmniRoute_Instance at or before the attempt's start time has ended, and while every Fleet_Inventory_Revision used for the attempt's attribution remains readable from version control, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL give the attempt the same attribution at every later computation, whatever Fleet_Inventory_Revisions are applied or rolled back afterward on any OmniRoute_Instance, and the same Device and Model_Offering, or the same Cloud_Target, as every other attributed attempt that records the same Target_Identifier (attribution stability property).
7. IF the OmniRoute_Instance ends an attempt, or a request for which it has made no attempt, without dispatching it to a Serving_Endpoint or Cloud_Target, THEN THE OmniRoute_Instance SHALL record an attempt for it that is marked as not dispatched, with an admission wait equal to its end-to-end latency, an upstream duration of 0, and an HTTP status that is not a 2xx success status.

### Requirement 18: Runtime anomaly detection

**User Story:** As the fleet operator, I want capacity leaks, dead targets, cap overruns, and rank inversions detected automatically, so that problems like the idle-but-busy 4070 Ti Super are caught in minutes instead of by forensic investigation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Managed_Instance has recorded two busy-slot skips (criterion 8) of one local target more than 5 minutes apart, and no attempt that the Managed_Instance dispatched to the target's Device or through the target's OmniRoute connection was in flight at any instant from the earlier skip through the later skip, THE Fleet_Observer SHALL raise a Capacity_Leak alert within 5 minutes after the records of that span are settled (criterion 8), naming:
   - the Managed_Instance, the target, and the target's Model_Offering and Device;
   - every Public_Route of that Managed_Instance whose Expanded_Target_Sequence, as Requirement 10 criterion 3 defines it, contains the target;
   - the completion time of the Managed_Instance's last attempt on that Device before the earlier skip, or the fact that its readable records contain none, and the number of busy-slot skips of the target from the earlier skip until the alert is raised.
2. ### Requirement 22: Change safety and secret handling

WHILE the Reconciler runs in dry-run mode, the Fleet_Observer computes metrics, a Preflight_Check runs, or the Arrangement_Evaluator evaluates an Arrangement, THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL send, for that activity, only read requests to OmniRoute_Instances and Serving_Endpoints, triggering no write (as criterion 2 defines it), model catalog synchronization, or inference request, so that the activity leaves every Live_Route_State and the set of models resident on each Serving_Endpoint unchanged, as Requirement 8.3 requires of drift detection.
2. THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL send writes only from the Reconciler, only during an apply (including the restore after a failed apply) or a rollback, and only to a Managed_Instance for which the invocation running that apply or rollback holds the per-invocation operator confirmation defined in criterion 7, sending no write to any Serving_Endpoint or to any other OmniRoute_Instance, where a write is any request that creates, updates, or deletes an object or setting of an OmniRoute_Instance or Serving_Endpoint (including a combo, connection, Routing_Setting, synced model catalog, or Published_Route_Metadata entry) or that triggers a model catalog synchronization, and an inference request is not a write.

THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL exclude every credential value, in full or in any masked, truncated, encoded, or other derived form, from every Desired_Route_State, Change_Plan, Route_Backup, report, log entry, alert, audit entry, error message, and other file or stored record that it writes, so that a credential value leaves the Fleet_Topology_System only in the requests of criterion 3, where:
   - a credential value is any value resolved from a Fleet_Inventory secret reference, any value that an OmniRoute_Instance or Serving_Endpoint returns in a field holding an API key, authentication token, password, or authorization header value, and the password in the user-information part of any URL;
   - an output that refers to a credential identifies it only by its secret reference, by a non-secret name that the OmniRoute_Instance assigns to it, or by a redaction marker that is the same for every credential value.
5. THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL replace each tokenized URL path segment, in every URL or URL path that a report, log entry, alert, audit entry, or error message contains, with a redaction marker that is the same for every replaced value, leaving the rest of the URL unchanged except as criterion 4 requires, where a tokenized URL path segment is the non-empty path segment that immediately follows the consecutive path segments `api`, `v1`, and `vscode`, in that order and compared case-insensitively, as `<token>` does in `/api/v1/vscode/<token>/`:
   - whatever its value, including a non-secret placeholder, unless the segment is a secret reference in the Fleet_Inventory format's secret-reference syntax;
   - whether or not further path segments, a query, or a fragment follow it.
6. THE Fleet_Topology_System SHALL copy no part of the body of an inference request (any request that asks a model to generate output, embed input, or rerank input) or of its response, including prompt, message, and system text, tool definitions, tool-call arguments and results, generated text, embedding and rerank inputs and outputs, and error message text returned for an inference request, into any Change_Plan, Route_Backup, report, alert, or audit entry, or into any log entry or error message that the Reconciler, Fleet_Observer, or Arrangement_Evaluator writes.
7. THE Reconciler SHALL accept, as the per-invocation operator confirmation of an apply or rollback on one Managed_Instance and as the deletion confirmation that Requirement 9.11 requires, only a confirmation that:
   - the operator supplies as input to that invocation before the invocation's first write, which excludes a confirmation supplied to an earlier invocation or recorded before the invocation started, for example in the Fleet_Inventory, a configuration setting, or an environment variable;
   - states the identity of the confirming operator;
   - names that Managed_Instance, so that an invocation that targets several Managed_Instances needs a separate confirmation for each;
   - identifies, for an apply, the Change_Plan by its Fleet_Inventory_Revision and creation time, or, for a rollback, the Route_Backup by its capture time.
8.
