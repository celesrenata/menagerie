-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION — KV path dtype-gated on weight/activation scheme: NO.
The SM120 NVFP4-KV kernel is gated ONLY on the KV-cache dtype (set by
`--kv-cache-dtype nvfp4`) and the GPU compute capability, independent of whether
the model loads as W4A4 or (auto-promoted) W4A16. Therefore the AxionML/Qwen3.5-9B-NVFP4
checkpoint — which vLLM may auto-promote weight-only NVFP4 to W4A16_NVFP4 at config
parse (see quant-evidence/vllm_modelopt.py lines 775-791, 842) — poses no problem for
`--kv-cache-dtype nvfp4` on the RTX 5090 (SM120). The remaining proof is the
OPERATOR GPU-live serve test (design §3.6 / plan item 20).
